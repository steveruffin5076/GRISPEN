THE SITUATION
The Ares Virus turned the world into a gray sketchbook. You are Neil, S.O.T. operative, left for dead after a traitor wrecked the mission. Re-arm. Gather. Craft. Survive each day — the infected grow faster, harder, and more numerous. The hut at the center of the woods is your shelter.

SURVIVAL

Chop stumps for wood · pick herb bushes · open crates · hunt critters for meat. Zombies drop Z-Coins and rotten meat. Beware: spiders poison you, vomiters throw acid, exploders blow up. Cook meat & make medicine at the shelter.
